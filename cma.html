Compensation, in Plain Terms

How the numbers on the previous page actually work.
The 2% / 3% / 4% tiers on the previous page are the listing side only — what you pay me to represent you and run the sale.
My recommendation: a 6% gross commission
To stay competitive with everything else a buyer's agent is showing, I recommend a 6% gross commission, split 50/50 with the buyer's broker — 3% to the listing side, 3% to the buyer's side. Offering a full co-op keeps your home on every buyer agent's list instead of quietly skipped.
